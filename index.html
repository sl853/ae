§ 02 — Guidance

When AI helps · when a simpler tool wins.

An honest model knows what it isn't for.

This is the part most AI products won't do.

Use AI when The task is shape, not fact.

  • Drafting & summarizing. Long emails, meeting notes, a first pass at a letter or a plan.
  • Translating shape. Turning bullet points into prose, prose into a checklist, one tone into another.
  • Thinking through a decision. Listing trade‑offs, pressure‑testing a plan, naming what's missing.
  • Reorganizing what you already have. Your notes, your files, your archive — restructured, not invented.

Use search instead when The task is fact, not shape.

  • Today's anything. Prices, weather, news, scores, opening hours. Models guess; pages know.
  • Public records & primary sources. Laws, datasets, official forms. Go to the source, not its echo.
  • Specific people, places, and products. Where the name and the number have to be exactly right.
  • Anything you'd want to cite. If the link matters, the link is the answer.
§ 02b — Who it's for

Built for real life, not enterprise scale.

Households and small businesses first. The roadmap stays bigger.
01 · Households

People who actually think about this

Consumers who already drive a Prius or Tesla, wear a Whoop or Oura, shop the farmers' market, use DuckDuckGo, and repair their Patagonia instead of replacing it. AI built to the same standard as everything else they buy.

02 · Small business

Teams that take privacy seriously

Independent practices, family-run operations, and boutique studios. People whose clients trust them not to leak their data into someone else's training set.

Eventually: schools, libraries, rural communities, emergency response. Right now: the households and small businesses that already care.
§ 03 — Update cadence

Slow updates by default.

Bandwidth is a resource. So is your attention.

Most users don't need a smarter model every week. Choose a rhythm that fits your bandwidth, your hardware, and your nerves.

Updates are signed bundles. Verify before you install. No silent changes, ever.
§ 04 — Device

Steward.

The local intelligence layer. Not a surveillance assistant.
A trusted local appliance

A quieter machine in the corner of the room.

Steward holds your model, your archive, and your update bundles locally. It connects to the cloud only when it announces it's about to — and only for what you asked.

Designed for households, studios, classrooms, clinics, and small operations who want intelligence without surrendering their data, their bandwidth, or their evenings.

  • Local‑first inference
  • Signed update bundles
  • Offline knowledge packs
  • Visible cloud handoff
  • Low‑power idle
  • Open audit log
