§ 00 — Who we are

A mission,
not a feature.

Tilth is built on a single belief: the next major opportunity in AI is not bigger models. It is trust, restraint, privacy, and responsible infrastructure.

Founded on the principle that AI should help people use AI — only when AI is actually the right answer
§ 01 — Founder thesis

The case for restraint.

Why we think the race to more AI is the wrong race.

The AI industry optimizes for engagement. More questions asked, more tokens generated, more cloud compute consumed. This is good for infrastructure companies and investors. It is not necessarily good for people.

Most AI products push users toward AI for everything — even when a calculator, a web search, a spreadsheet, or a phone call would be faster, more accurate, and far less energy-intensive.

The environmental cost of each interaction is invisible. The privacy cost is buried in terms of service. The bandwidth cost is absorbed silently. The data dependency is treated as normal.

Tilth is built on the belief that the next major AI opportunity is not more AI — it is appropriate AI. Products that are honest about when AI helps and when it doesn't. Systems that show their resource cost rather than hiding it. Infrastructure that works offline, updates on your schedule, and never moves your data without telling you.

This is AI for people who want intelligence without surrendering privacy, money, attention, or environmental responsibility.

§ 02 — The problem

What current AI gets wrong.

Not a critique of AI. A critique of how it's deployed.
01 · Dependence

Cloud‑dependent by default

Every question leaves your home. Every answer requires a data center. Every interaction is a network call you didn't choose to make.

02 · Cost

Energy‑intensive and opaque

A single cloud AI response can use more energy than a web search by orders of magnitude. Users never see this. They are never asked to care.

03 · Trust

Updates without announcement

The model you used last week is different from the model you use today. No notification. No changelog. No way to verify what changed.

04 · Engagement

AI is pushed for everything

Products optimize for AI engagement. They never tell you that a web search, a calculator, or a direct database would be more accurate and use far less compute.

05 · Privacy

Data moves without notice

What gets sent to the cloud? What is stored? For how long? These questions have answers buried in policy documents most users never read.

06 · Resilience

Fails without connectivity

Rural areas, low-bandwidth environments, emergencies, and off-grid settings are treated as edge cases. AI that requires constant internet is not infrastructure.

§ 03 — The answer

Build an AI that is honest about its own use.

Not more AI. Better AI, used deliberately.

What Tilth does differently

Tilth routes requests to the least expensive, most appropriate answer — whether that's local AI, web search, or cloud reasoning. It tells you which it used and why.

It shows the estimated environmental impact of each response. Not to create guilt, but to create awareness. Informed use is better use.

It updates on your schedule, with signed bundles you can verify. It works offline. It keeps an open audit log. It tells you when to use search instead of AI.

The core principles

  • Privacy first. Personal data stays local. Cloud use is optional, visible, and justified.
  • Low-energy by default. Lightweight local answers preferred unless deeper reasoning is needed.
  • Transparent compute. Every request shows its routing, privacy status, and resource impact.
  • Slow updates. Signed bundles on your schedule. Weekly by default. Manual if you prefer.
  • Offline-first resilience. Local knowledge packs. No constant internet dependency.
  • Honest routing. Tilth tells you when search, a calculator, or a direct source is better.

§ 05 — Infrastructure vision

Tilth as a distribution layer.

Long-term · beyond the device

Steward is the first product. Tilth is the layer beneath it — a low-bandwidth, one-way distribution network for responsible AI updates, knowledge packs, and civic information.

The goal is to reduce unnecessary repeated cloud calls and create a resilient distribution system for communities, devices, and institutions that cannot depend on constant connectivity.

Think of it as a postal system for AI. Signed. Verified. Scheduled. Addressed to specific communities with specific needs. Not a firehose of continuous updates, but a considered, deliberate flow of intelligence.

What the network delivers
01
Signed model patches — incremental updates, cryptographically verified
02
Knowledge packs — curated local and domain-specific information bundles
03
Offline maps — local geographic data for disconnected use
04
Public safety updates — emergency broadcast and civic information
05
Education packs — curriculum-aligned knowledge for schools and libraries
06
Civic information — local government, services, and public records
07
Low-bandwidth AI updates — designed for rural and intermittent connectivity
§ 06 — Values

What we actually believe.

Design decisions follow from values, not the other way around.
01 · Restraint

Appropriate use over maximum use

We optimize for the right answer, not the most AI answer. If search is better, we say so. If a calculator is better, we route there. We do not optimize for engagement.

02 · Honesty

Visible cost, visible routing

Every request shows its routing decision, privacy exposure, and estimated environmental impact. We do not hide what AI costs to run.

03 · Privacy

Local by default, cloud by choice

Personal data stays on your device unless you decide otherwise. Cloud use is visible, announced, and justified. No silent transfers.

04 · Resilience

Works when the internet doesn't

AI that requires constant connectivity is not infrastructure. Tilth is designed to work offline, update slowly, and function in low-bandwidth environments.

05 · Trust

Verified, signed, auditable

Every update is a signed bundle. Every request is logged locally. Every routing decision is visible. You should be able to verify what your AI is doing.

06 · Scale that fits

Human-scale intelligence

This is not a race to build the biggest model. It is a system for daily life — writing, planning, learning, archiving, and decision-making at a human scale.
