§ 02 — Features

What Steward actually does.

Designed around what most AI systems refuse to offer.
01

Local‑first inference

Answers your questions using a model stored on the device itself. No cloud round-trip, no latency, no data leaving your home.

Privacy · high
02

Signed update bundles

Model updates arrive as cryptographically signed packages. Verify before install. Weekly cadence by default, monthly or manual if you prefer.

Verified · always
03

Offline knowledge packs

Curated knowledge sets — medical basics, civic information, local maps, education packs — stored on‑device and updated on your schedule.

Works offline
04

Visible cloud handoff

When Steward needs to escalate to cloud AI, it tells you before it does — what it's sending, why, and how to cancel. No silent data transfers.

Transparent
05

Responsible compute panel

Every response shows its estimated privacy exposure, energy use, water use, and carbon impact. Numbers are estimates, not marketing. Informed use, not guilt.

Impact · visible
06

Open audit log

A local, human-readable record of every request, routing decision, and cloud handoff. Yours to read, export, or delete at any time.

Open · auditable
07

Low‑power idle

When not in use, Steward draws minimal power. No always-on cloud polling, no background model updates without your permission.

Energy · low
08

Smart routing

Built-in intent classifier decides whether a request should go to local AI, web search, cloud AI, or a direct database — and explains why.

Routing · visible
09

Private archive

Store your own documents, notes, and files locally. Ask questions about them without uploading anything to a third-party server.

Data · yours
§ 03 — How it works

A request, step by step.

Nothing happens without Steward telling you what it's doing.
01

You ask

Type or speak your question. Steward classifies it instantly: shape task, factual lookup, calculation, or deep reasoning.

02

Steward routes

Local AI, web search, or cloud — chosen based on the question, not the default. You see the routing decision and can override it.

03

Impact is shown

Privacy, energy, water, and carbon estimates appear for each response. Not as warnings — as information.

04

Logged locally

The request, routing decision, and response are written to your local audit log. No external telemetry. No data sold.
